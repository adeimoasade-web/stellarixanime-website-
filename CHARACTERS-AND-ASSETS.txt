STARFORGED WEBSITE PACKAGE

Character gallery uses the four established THE LAST STAR characters:
- Kairo — Seventh Star energy
- Reina — lightning
- Zane — wind
- Lord Vexar — shadows

The individual character portrait files are crops from the approved STARFORGED cast poster supplied for this project. They preserve the source anime artwork; no new character designs or names have been invented.
